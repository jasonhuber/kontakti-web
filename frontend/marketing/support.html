Support

Kontakti help and account assistance

Contact support

Email jason@kontakti.app. Include the platform you use, what you expected, what happened, and any relevant error message. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or sensitive contact data.

Account and sign-in

You can sign in at kontakti.app/app. If you can sign in but need to remove your account, open Settings and choose Delete account.

Google connections

Linked Google accounts can be managed from Settings. Unlinking an account stops future synchronization from that connection; it does not automatically delete contacts already imported into Kontakti.

Delete your account

See the complete instructions and deletion scope on the account deletion page.
