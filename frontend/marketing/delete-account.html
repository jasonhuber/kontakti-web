Permanent account and data deletion

Delete in Kontakti

  1. Sign in to the Kontakti web app, iOS app, or Android app.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Type DELETE and confirm.

This action cannot be undone. It removes your Kontakti profile, contacts, companies, discussions, notes, tasks, tags, relationship activity, linked integrations, notification tokens, authentication tokens, and uploaded contact photos.

If you cannot sign in

Email jason@kontakti.app from the address associated with your account. We will verify ownership before processing the request. Never send your password.

Third-party data

Deleting Kontakti does not delete data held independently by Google, Apple, your device contacts, or other third-party services. You can separately revoke Kontakti's access in the relevant provider's account settings.

For more information, read the Privacy Policy.
