Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

Kontakti is a personal relationship manager. This policy explains what information the hosted Kontakti service processes and the choices available to you.

Information you provide

  • Account information such as your name, username, email address, and authentication credentials.
  • Contact and relationship information you add or import, including names, contact details, notes, companies, activities, reminders, photos, and audio you choose to submit.
  • Google account data you authorize Kontakti to access, such as contacts and email-related information used by features you request.
  • Device and notification tokens used to deliver notifications.

How information is used

We use your information to provide, secure, maintain, and improve Kontakti; synchronize your data across signed-in devices; perform imports and integrations you request; provide reminders and notifications; and respond to support requests. We do not sell your personal information.

Google user data

Kontakti's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You control which Google accounts are linked and may unlink them in Settings.

Service providers and disclosure

Information may be processed by hosting, authentication, notification, and integration providers only as needed to operate the service. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect the service or its users, or as part of a business transfer with appropriate safeguards.

Security and retention

Kontakti uses access controls and encrypted network connections. No system is completely secure. We retain information while your account is active and as needed to provide the service, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and protect the service.

Your choices

You may review and edit your data in the app, unlink Google accounts, export information through available features, or permanently delete your account. Account deletion removes your profile and Kontakti-hosted account data. See Delete your account.

Children

Kontakti is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will revise the effective date above. Questions can be sent to jason@kontakti.app.
